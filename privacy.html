PRIVACY & SAFETY

Protect patient information.

Private cases

Case and payment records are not public.

Private receipts

Receipt files should be stored in a private bucket and accessible only to authorized staff.

Doctor access

Use individual accounts, MFA, role-based access and audit logs.

Emergency pathway

Emergency symptoms should be directed to emergency care instead of normal processing.

This is a technical starter. Obtain appropriate security, privacy, professional and legal review before accepting real patient information.
