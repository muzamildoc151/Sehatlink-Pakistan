PRIVACY & SAFETY

Patient information should be treated as sensitive.

This page is a launch checklist, not a final legal privacy policy.

Collect less

Only collect information needed to handle the request. Avoid CNIC numbers and financial credentials.

Secure the case

Use authenticated accounts, role-based access, encrypted transport, secure storage and audit logs in the production backend.

Separate website from records

Do not store patient histories in GitHub, HTML, JavaScript files, public spreadsheets or browser local storage.

Verify the team

Publish accurate professional information and use verified registration details before marketing the service.

Important

The current GitHub-ready version is a prototype. The form does not transmit or store the submitted medical history. Do not accept real patient data until a secure backend, access controls, consent wording and appropriate professional/legal review are in place.
