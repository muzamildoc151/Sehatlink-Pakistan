THE WORKFLOW

Human review, clear handoff.

Our MVP is designed to move a patient from uncertainty to an appropriate point of care.

01

Patient request

The patient pays the service fee and submits a short history with location and contact details.

02

Case assignment

A private case ID is created. One member of the four-doctor team takes ownership and the assignment is logged.

03

Follow-up questions

The assigned doctor can contact the patient through the verified communication channel if important information is missing.

04

Specialty direction

The team identifies the most appropriate type of clinician to see. This is healthcare navigation, not a definitive diagnosis.

05

Nearby options

The production version will search a verified doctor/hospital directory by specialty and location and present suitable options.

Before launch

Because the service handles health information and may involve remote clinician-patient interaction, have the final workflow, consent, professional scope, data security, payment model and referral process reviewed for compliance in Pakistan. The Ministry's published telemedicine policy document describes secure platforms and electronic medical-record capabilities for telemedicine settings, while PM&DC publishes current laws/regulations and practitioner information.
